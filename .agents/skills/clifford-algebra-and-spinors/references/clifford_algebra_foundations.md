# Clifford Algebra Foundations: $\mathcal{Cl}(p, q, r)$

This reference provides the rigorous mathematical formulation of real Clifford algebras $\mathcal{Cl}(p, q, r)$ in $n$ dimensions ($n = p + q + r$).

---

## 1. Defining Relations & Signatures

Let $V$ be an $n$-dimensional real vector space equipped with a symmetric bilinear form $Q: V \times V \to \mathbb{R}$ of signature $(p, q, r)$, where:
- $p$ basis vectors square to $+1$ (space-like)
- $q$ basis vectors square to $-1$ (time-like)
- $r$ basis vectors square to $0$ (degenerate / projective null)

The Clifford algebra $\mathcal{Cl}(p, q, r)$ is the associative unital algebra generated by $V$ subject to the fundamental Clifford relation:

$$u v + v u = 2 Q(u, v) \mathbf{1}, \quad \forall u, v \in V$$

For an orthogonal basis $\{e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n\}$:

$$e_i e_j + e_j e_i = 2 \eta_{ij} \mathbf{1}, \quad \eta_{ij} = \begin{cases} +1 & 1 \le i \le p \\ -1 & p < i \le p + q \\ 0 & p + q < i \le n \end{cases}$$

---

## 2. Multivector Space & Grade Decomposition

The algebra $\mathcal{Cl}(p, q, r)$ has total dimension $2^n$ as a real vector space. Any multivector $A \in \mathcal{Cl}(p, q, r)$ decomposes uniquely into homogeneous grade components:

$$A = \sum_{k=0}^n \langle A \rangle_k = \langle A \rangle_0 + \langle A \rangle_1 + \langle A \rangle_2 + \dots + \langle A \rangle_n$$

| Grade $k$ | Entity Type | Basis Count $\binom{n}{k}$ | Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **0** | Scalar | $\binom{n}{0} = 1$ | $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$ |
| **1** | Vector | $\binom{n}{1} = n$ | $e_1, e_2, \dots, e_n$ |
| **2** | Bivector (2-blade) | $\binom{n}{2}$ | $e_1 e_2, e_1 e_3, \dots$ |
| **$k$** | $k$-vector ($k$-blade) | $\binom{n}{k}$ | $e_{i_1} e_{i_2} \dots e_{i_k}$ |
| **$n$** | Pseudoscalar | $\binom{n}{n} = 1$ | $I_n = e_1 e_2 \dots e_n$ |

---

## 3. Fundamental Products & Operators

1. **Geometric Product**:
   $$u v = u \cdot v + u \wedge v$$
2. **Inner Product (Left Contraction $\rfloor$)**:
   $$\langle A \rfloor B \rangle = \langle A B \rangle_{s - r} \quad (r = \text{grade}(A), s = \text{grade}(B), r \le s)$$
3. **Outer / Wedge Product ($\wedge$)**:
   $$\langle A \wedge B \rangle = \langle A B \rangle_{r + s}$$
4. **Commutator Product ($\times$)**:
   $$A \times B = \frac{1}{2}(A B - B A)$$

---

## 4. Fundamental Involutions

For a basis blade $e_{i_1} e_{i_2} \dots e_{i_k}$:

1. **Reversion ($\tilde{A}$ / $A^\dagger$)**: Reverses the order of vectors in every blade:
   $$\widetilde{e_{i_1} e_{i_2} \dots e_{i_k}} = e_{i_k} \dots e_{i_2} e_{i_1} = (-1)^{\frac{k(k-1)}{2}} e_{i_1} \dots e_{i_k}$$
   - Grade 0, 1: unchanged ($\tilde{\alpha} = \alpha$, $\tilde{v} = v$)
   - Grade 2, 3: negated ($\tilde{B} = -B$, $\tilde{T} = -T$)
2. **Grade Involution ($\hat{A}$)**: Parity reflection $v \mapsto -v$:
   $$\widehat{e_{i_1} e_{i_2} \dots e_{i_k}} = (-1)^k e_{i_1} e_{i_2} \dots e_{i_k}$$
   - Divides algebra into Even Subalgebra $\mathcal{Cl}^+$ and Odd Subspace $\mathcal{Cl}^-$.
3. **Clifford Conjugation ($\bar{A}$)**: Composition of reversion and grade involution:
   $$\bar{A} = \widehat{\widetilde{A}} = \widetilde{\widehat{A}} = \sum_{k=0}^n (-1)^{\frac{k(k+1)}{2}} \langle A \rangle_k$$

## 5. Standard Low-Dimensional Isomorphisms

| Algebra | Geometric Interpretation | Classical Matrix / Division Ring Isomorphism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(0, 1)$ | Dual / Complex numbers | $\mathbb{C}$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(0, 2)$ | Quaternions | $\mathbb{H}$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(3, 0)$ | 3D Euclidean Geometric Algebra (VGA / Pauli) | $\mathbb{C}(2) \cong \text{Mat}(2, \mathbb{C})$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}^+(3, 0)$ | 3D Rotors / Quaternions | $\mathbb{H}$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(1, 3)$ | Spacetime Algebra (STA, Dirac particle) | $\mathbb{H}(2) \cong \text{Mat}(2, \mathbb{H}) \cong \text{Mat}(4, \mathbb{C})$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(3, 1)$ | Spacetime Algebra (Majorana) | $\text{Mat}(4, \mathbb{R})$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(4, 1)$ | 5D Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA) | $\text{Mat}(4, \mathbb{C}) \cong \mathbb{C}(4)$ |
| $\mathcal{Cl}(3, 0, 1)$ | 3D Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA / Motors) | $\mathbb{H}[\epsilon] / (\epsilon^2 = 0)$ (Dual Quaternions) |
