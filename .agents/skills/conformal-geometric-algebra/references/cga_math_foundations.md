# Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA): Mathematical Foundations

This reference provides the rigorous mathematical framework for Conformal Geometric Algebra in $n$ dimensions ($\text{CGA}_n = \mathcal{G}(n+1, 1)$), focusing primarily on 5D CGA ($\mathcal{G}(4, 1)$ for 3D space).

---

## 1. Geometric Algebra Basics

Let $V = \mathbb{R}^{p, q}$ be a pseudo-Euclidean vector space with signature $(p, q)$. The Geometric Algebra $\mathcal{G}(p, q)$ is generated by the geometric product of vectors:

$$uv = u \cdot v + u \wedge v$$

where:
- **Inner product (symmetric)**: $u \cdot v = \frac{1}{2}(uv + vu) \in \mathbb{R}$
- **Outer / Wedge product (antisymmetric)**: $u \wedge v = \frac{1}{2}(uv - vu) \in \bigwedge^2 V$ (bivector)

Two orthogonal vectors anticommute: $u \perp v \implies uv = -vu = u \wedge v$.

---

## 2. Conformal Embedding $\mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^{n+1, 1}$

To represent conformal transformations (isometries, dilations, and spherical inversions) linearly as versors, Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$ is embedded into a space with two additional dimensions:
- One space-like basis vector: $e_+$ where $e_+^2 = +1$
- One time-like basis vector: $e_-$ where $e_-^2 = -1$

### The Null Basis ($e_0, e_\infty$)
Instead of $(e_+, e_-)$, CGA uses the null basis vectors representing the **origin** ($e_0$) and **infinity** ($e_\infty$):

$$e_0 = \frac{1}{2}(e_- - e_+), \quad e_\infty = e_- + e_+$$

#### Fundamental Properties:
$$e_0^2 = 0, \quad e_\infty^2 = 0, \quad e_0 \cdot e_\infty = -1, \quad e_0 \wedge e_\infty = e_- e_+$$

The Minkowski pseudoscalar of the null subspace is:
$$E = e_\infty \wedge e_0 = e_+ e_-$$
$$E^2 = 1, \quad e_0 E = -e_0, \quad e_\infty E = e_\infty$$

---

## 3. Conformal Point Representation

An arbitrary Euclidean vector $\mathbf{x} = \sum_{i=1}^n x_i e_i \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is mapped to a null vector $P(\mathbf{x}) \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1, 1}$:

$$P(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbf{x} + \frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{x}\|^2 e_\infty + e_0$$

### Key Properties:
1. **Null Vector Invariant**:
   $$P(\mathbf{x})^2 = P(\mathbf{x}) \cdot P(\mathbf{x}) = \|\mathbf{x}\|^2 + 2 \left(\frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{x}\|^2\right)(e_\infty \cdot e_0) = \|\mathbf{x}\|^2 - \|\mathbf{x}\|^2 = 0$$
2. **Euclidean Distance Metric**:
   $$P(\mathbf{x}) \cdot P(\mathbf{y}) = -\frac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{y}\|^2$$
3. **Normalization**:
   Any homogeneous conformal vector $X$ with $X \cdot e_\infty \neq 0$ is normalized to standard form via:
   $$\hat{X} = \frac{X}{-X \cdot e_\infty}$$

---

## 4. Versor Algebra & Transformations

In CGA, all conformal maps are represented as sandwich operations $X' = V X V^{-1}$ where $V$ is a product of invertible vectors (a versor):

| Transformation | Versor $V$ | Generator / Form | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Translation** by $\mathbf{t}$ | $T = 1 - \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{t} e_\infty$ | $\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\mathbf{t} e_\infty\right)$ | $T^{-1} = 1 + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{t} e_\infty$ |
| **Rotation** by $\theta$ in plane $B$ | $R = \cos\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right) - B \sin\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)$ | $\exp\left(-\frac{\theta}{2} B\right)$ | $B^2 = -1$ |
| **Dilation / Scale** by $\gamma$ | $D = \frac{1+\gamma}{2\sqrt{\gamma}} + \frac{1-\gamma}{2\sqrt{\gamma}} E$ | $\exp\left(-\frac{1}{2}\ln(\gamma) E\right)$ | Uniform scaling around origin |
| **Spherical Inversion** (radius $R$) | $S = e_0 - \frac{1}{2}R^2 e_\infty$ | Vector reflection $X' = -S X S^{-1}$ | Exact geometric involution |
| **Motor (Rigid Motion)** | $M = T R$ | Product of translator and rotor | 6-DOF Euclidean transform |

---

## 5. Duality & The Meet Operator

Given the pseudoscalar $I_{5} = e_1 e_2 e_3 e_0 e_\infty$ (with $I_{5}^2 = -1$):
- **Dual of a multivector $A$**: $A^* = A I_5^{-1} = -A I_5$
- **Meet (Intersection)** of two geometric entities $A$ and $B$:
  $$A \vee B = (A^* \wedge B^*)^*$$
- **Outer Product Null Space (OPNS)**: Entity $A$ represents all points $x$ such that $x \wedge A = 0$.
- **Inner Product Null Space (IPNS)**: Entity $A^*$ represents all points $x$ such that $x \cdot A^* = 0$.
