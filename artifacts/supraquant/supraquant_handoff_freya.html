01 Greeting

Dear Freya,

This page is a concise technical handoff of an experimental closed spline construction we have been calling SupraQuant. It was developed with your 2022 talk The Continuity of Splines (and your later public experiments with higher-degree interpolating C² curves) as the primary design brief.

The goal was to package a sine-ratio Cox–de Boor analogue (in the trigonometric B-spline family) with strict 2π periodicity, collocation pre-filtering, and live curvature/jerk diagnostics — aimed at interpolation, local control, and higher continuity in an interactive closed-curve setting.

Nothing here claims a novel mathematical foundation — trigonometric B-splines already exist. It is offered as a working prototype and diagnostic sandbox; the interesting part is the interactive continuity instrumentation and the packaging for closed curves.

02 The gap you described

Your video and subsequent posts repeatedly surface a practical tension that is still not fully resolved in everyday tools:

Cubic B-spline

Reliable C² + local control, but approximating — does not pass through the control points.

Catmull-Rom

Interpolates and stays local, but stops at C¹. Acceleration / curvature usually discontinuous.

Natural cubic

Interpolates and reaches C², at the cost of global coupling — move one point, the whole curve shifts.

Higher-degree polys

Can force more continuity, yet “fairness” (smooth, low-energy, non-stiff shapes) becomes difficult.

SupraQuant sits in that region via a known trigonometric B-spline style basis plus collocation pre-filtering, with an emphasis on closed curves and live continuity diagnostics rather than a claim of a new spline space.

03 What SupraQuant is

At its core it is a periodic cubic-degree trigonometric B-spline style basis (related to classical trigonometric B-splines) whose ratios replace the usual linear Cox–de Boor terms with normalised sines of angular differences. A scale factor keeps arguments away from integer multiples of π. Strict 2π-periodicity is enforced by normalising the evaluation angle at every recursion level.

Because the raw basis is approximating, a dense collocation matrix is inverted so virtual control points make the curve pass through user targets. Partition-of-unity is restored by renormalising active weights.

Basis

evaluateSupraQuantBasis — sine-corrected Cox–de Boor with θ forced into [0, 2π).

Weights

getSupraQuantWeights — four active bases, periodic wrap, renormalise ∑w = 1.

Pre-filter

solvePreFilteringMatrix — collocation B·Cvirtual = Dtarget.

Diagnostics

Live curvature combs, acceleration magnitude, and jerk / jolt plots over [0, 2π].

05 Novelty (honest)

Trigonometric B-splines (Schoenberg and a large modern literature), periodic closed constructions, and collocation pre-filtering are already known. SupraQuant is not a claim of a new spline space.

What may still be useful:

Packaging

Strict 2π normalisation at every recursion level + partition-of-unity restore for interactive closed curves.

Diagnostics

Live curvature combs and jerk plots in the same visual language as The Continuity of Splines.

Sandbox

Side-by-side linear / Catmull / B-spline / trig modes for falsifying continuity claims in real time.

Open questions

Fairness vs classical trig B-splines; analytic derivatives; local interpolating variants without a global matrix.

06 Why it may still interest you

You have explored the same design space — most recently an interpolating C² quintic with local control. SupraQuant is a practical packaging of a sine-ratio periodic basis + collocation + live continuity diagnostics, not a different fundamental theory.

If the construction is a dead end, the failure modes may still be informative. If the diagnostics help stress-test continuity claims, that alone is useful. — revised handoff note
